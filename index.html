Why this verdict

    How the score works

    Each area is rated 0–10 and weighted: Safety record 20%, Licensing & permits 15%, Labor & worker rights 20%, Anti-bribery & conflicts of interest 15%, Environmental compliance 10%, Payment & contract integrity 10%, Complaint history 10%. The weighted total is scaled to 100.

    Verdict: Ethical at 75 or above, Needs review from 50 to 74, Not ethical below 50. Any confirmed critical red flag (bribery, forged documents, child or forced labor, fatal safety violation) makes the verdict Not ethical regardless of score.
